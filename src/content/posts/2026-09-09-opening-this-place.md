Liking something falls apart more easily than you would think.

There was a film I loved last month. Now only the title remains. I think I held my
breath during one scene, and I think I didn't want to speak for a while after
walking out of the theatre. That is all that is left.

So I made this place. Not to write anything remarkable — more to catch the good
things before they disappear. A few lines is fine. A single photograph is fine.

## What will collect here

- Thoughts and diary entries
- Films, books, records that stayed with me
- The air of places I've been
- Notes on using my body

## Something like rules

One. Never let trying to write well turn into not writing at all.
Two. Don't explain to anyone — speak to a later version of myself.
Three. Leave the unfinished pieces where they are.

> I don't write things down to remember them. I write them down so it's alright
> to forget.

That's enough for now. The real stories start with the next one.
